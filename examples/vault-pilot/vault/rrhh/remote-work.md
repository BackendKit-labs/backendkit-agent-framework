# Remote work policy

Office roles may work remotely up to **three days a week**. Warehouse roles are on site.

## Rules

- Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 in the local time zone.
- Remote days are agreed with your manager and written in the team calendar.
- The company provides a laptop and a monitor. A home-office allowance of 40 EUR per month is paid with the payroll.

## Working from another country

Working from another country for more than two weeks needs written approval from HR, because of tax and social security rules.
