# Payroll calendar

Salaries are paid on the **25th** of each month. When the 25th is not a working day, they are paid on the previous working day.

## Cut-off

Changes to a payslip (new bank account, overtime, allowances) must reach Finance by the **15th**. Later changes apply the following month.

## Corrections

An error in a payslip is corrected in the next payroll, or in an extra payment within five working days if the difference is above 200 EUR.
