# Discount policy

Sales representatives may give a discount of up to **10%** on the list price without approval.

## Above 10%

- 11% to 20%: the sales manager approves.
- Above 20%: the commercial director approves, and the deal needs a written reason.

## Rules

Discounts are valid for 30 days from the quote date. Multi-year deals follow the price list for multi-year contracts.
