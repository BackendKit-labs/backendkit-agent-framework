# Refund process for customers

A customer may ask for a refund within **30 days** of delivery.

## Steps

1. Check the order and the delivery date in the order system.
2. If the goods are damaged, refund in full and open a claim with the carrier.
3. If the customer changed their mind, refund the goods and deduct the return shipping.
4. Refunds above 300 EUR need the approval of the support supervisor.

## Tone

Apologise once, say what will happen and when. Do not promise dates that depend on the carrier.
