# Parental leave policy

Every employee with six months of service is entitled to parental leave.

## Length

- The birth parent takes **16 weeks** of paid leave.
- The other parent takes **8 weeks** of paid leave.
- Adoption follows the same rules as birth.

## How to ask

1. Tell your manager at least eight weeks before the planned start, when you can.
2. File the leave request with HR. HR confirms the dates within five working days.
3. Leave can start up to four weeks before the expected date.

## Pay

Pay is 100% of base salary during the whole leave. Bonuses are paid in proportion to the time worked.
