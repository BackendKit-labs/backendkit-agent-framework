# Expense approval limits

Who may approve an expense depends on the amount.

| Amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 500 EUR | Your team lead |
| 501 to 5,000 EUR | The department head |
| Above 5,000 EUR | The CFO, with a written quote |

## Rules

- Split purchases to stay under a limit are treated as one purchase.
- Nobody approves their own expense or one from their direct manager.
- Receipts are uploaded within 30 days of the purchase.
