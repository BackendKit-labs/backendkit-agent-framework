# Company overview

Acme Logistics runs the warehouse network for regional retailers. About 240 people work in four sites.

## Working hours

The office is open from 8:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. Warehouse shifts run in three blocks: 6:00-14:00, 14:00-22:00 and 22:00-6:00.

## Public holidays

The company follows the national calendar and adds one extra day, the Friday after the founders' day, every year.

## Who to ask

- People matters: the HR team (folder rrhh).
- Money matters: the Finance team (folder finanzas).
- Customer problems: the Support team (folder soporte).
