# Data handling policy

This policy applies to every department.

## Personal data

- Personal data of employees and customers is used only for the task it was given for.
- Never paste personal data into tools outside the company systems.
- Salaries, health data and national identifiers are confidential: share them only with the people who need them for their work.

## Retention

Customer records are kept for six years. Employee records are kept for the length of the contract plus five years.

## Incidents

Report any suspected leak to the security team within 24 hours.
